## Result

K1.6 is **blocked**, not closed. More precisely, literal instantiation of BCS
2016, Theorem 7.1 is a **no-go** for the deployed transcript. BCS is the proof
blueprint, not a theorem that can be applied verbatim: its Section-6 compiler
uses a different full-output linear hash/Merkle chain, whereas Tag-73 uses
framed absorbs, the independent pair `H(S || 01)` and `H(S || 02)`, three
same-oracle nonce searches, 208-bit typed trees, bounded samplers, and a cloned
first-cap-203 query forest. Consequently the published
`3(Q^2+1)/2^256` loss is not yet a Tag-73 term.

The work now kernel-checks five independent pieces:

1. a fixed-table deterministic replay of the exact Tag-73 duplex/query
   grammar, including stage-local work erasure and cloned q16 branches;
2. executable bounded ordinary-QM31, nonzero-QM31, and q16 decoders with exact
   block caps and first-occurrence ordering;
3. the exact secure-circle rational map over the deployed QM31 tower,
   including singular-before-subfield rejection and byte-representation
   conversion;
4. a start-only same-hidden-tape operational replay construction that derives
   its pause by replaying a recorded prefix rather than accepting an opaque
   adversary checkpoint; and
5. exact finite-uniform `2^256` point, finite-target, collision, and union
   counting lemmas.

These pieces intentionally stop before the cryptographic conclusion. A
nonempty adversary-program pause is not automatically a complete previously
seen state of a ROM-free interactive verifier. The immediate deterministic
gap is named `Tag73QueryDagToCompleteVerifierStateMapNeeded`. The remaining
distributional work is an adaptive lazy-oracle/query-forest bound;
`AdaptiveHashChainProbabilityLemmaNeeded` records only its BCS-shaped
benchmark in
[`V7FsStateRestorationCoupling.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7FsStateRestorationCoupling.lean).
They name two components of the still-unproved protocol-specific replacement
for BCS Lemma 7.3; neither definition establishes its target proposition.

The plain compiler-side raw-error formula is now a kernel-visible sum of six
event probabilities in
[`V7Tag73PlainRawError.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7Tag73PlainRawError.lean).
It contains no BCS constant, C2 penalty, work factor, sampler-completeness
event, simulator term, or WUR term. A concrete coupling must instantiate the
sets before the probabilities are actual Tag-73 errors. The accounting does
not prove that Tag-73 acceptance is covered by the six-event union; that
inclusion is part of the missing coupling lemma.

The older `Tag73FreshAcceptanceTraceCover` hypothesis in
[`V7FsAokCompiler.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7FsAokCompiler.lean)
still proves only conditional probability arithmetic. It is not accepted as
closure, because supplying that cover would merely rename the missing
compiler bridge. A formal countermodel in that file correctly proves that an
interactive K1.2–K1.5 theorem alone cannot imply a noninteractive bound.

If the missing constructive coupling is eventually proved, the intended
plain-ROM composition is:

```text
Pr[Tag73 acceptance]
  ≤ L · Pr[valid extracted witness] + εTag73,plain,raw(C)

(Pr[Tag73 acceptance] - εTag73,plain,raw(C)) / L
  ≤ Pr[valid extracted witness]                 (0 < L < ∞).
```

These are probabilistic statements. Nothing claims that every individual
accepting proof deterministically yields a witness. Observed/simulated-proof
security is a separate, stronger theorem and additionally requires canonical
simulation, programming freshness, and weak unique response; none of those
terms belongs in the plain theorem.

## Exact SHA duplex

Let `S` be the current 32-byte transcript state, initially zero. The deployed
primitive is:

```text
absorb(label, data): S := SHA256(S || 0x00 || label || data)
squeeze block:       out := SHA256(S || 0x01)
                     S   := SHA256(S || 0x02)
grind(nonceLE):      SHA256(S || 0x03 || nonceLE)  -- S is unchanged
```

Every challenge sampler consumes a sequential stream of squeeze blocks; a
rejected field word advances that same stream. Thus a challenge is bound to
the complete preceding transcript state, but challenge calls are not assumed
independent.

The ordinary QM31 sampler uses at most four blocks: four limbs, eight candidate
31-bit words per limb, eight words per block. The nonzero-QM31 and secure-circle
samplers each make at most three outer attempts and therefore consume at most
twelve blocks. Sampler exhaustion is an explicit verifier rejection and
honest-completeness event, not an unbounded ideal sample or an automatic
plain extraction-loss term.

[`V7Tag73SamplerDecoder.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7Tag73SamplerDecoder.lean)
implements those byte decoders and the 64-draw q16 scan. The secure-circle
parameter is completed in
[`V7Tag73SecureCircleMap.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7Tag73SecureCircleMap.lean)
using the exact deployed tower. The remaining source-level statement that a
Rust `u32 & (2^k-1)` is this arithmetic low part belongs to the named
Charon/Aeneas correspondence boundary; the exact tower already proves the
mathematical low-31 bit-and identity.

## Binding and retry claims proved in Lean

[`V7Tag73TranscriptSchedule.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7Tag73TranscriptSchedule.lean)
proves syntactically, before any cryptographic assumption:

- every absorb hashes the exact previous 32-byte state, domain byte, label, and
  payload;
- every squeeze hashes the exact previous state under `0x01` and advances it
  under `0x02`;
- grinding hashes `state || 0x03 || nonceLE` without advancing `state`;
- each challenge starts from the state obtained by running exactly its event
  prefix;
- C2 absorption occurs after `lambda` and `chi`;
- the three work stages are separate and none has a first-success policy;
- every earlier q16 counter is sampled and noncompact, while the chosen
  schedule is compact;
- all counter branches start from one post-final-nonce base, and only the
  selected branch continues;
- `rho` is sampled from the state after the selected branch; and
- terminal query acceptance exposes only the one batched residual equality.

Concluding that two different byte histories cannot share a state is exactly
the SHA-256/random-oracle boundary; it is not asserted by these syntactic
theorems.

The schedule module alone fixes query inputs and recorded block counts; it
does not equate a decoded challenge with those blocks. `TraceWellFormed` in
`V7Tag73DeterministicRefinement` adds that link, and
`exactDeterministicDecoders` supplies the executable ordinary/nonzero/circle
and q16 decoding functions. Each circle challenge retains both its accepted
16-byte parameter and the separate 32-byte `(x,y)` Rust return. This excludes
the schedule-only altered-`lambda` countermodel proved in
`V7BcsApplicabilityGate` once `TraceWellFormed` is supplied.

## Observed-proof extraction experiment

[`V7FsAokExperiment.lean`](../../AspisFormal/AspisFormal/K1/V7FsAokExperiment.lean)
contains the operational game, not a one-proof decoder:

- one finite master PMF fixes all first-run, simulator, verifier, replay, and
  extractor coins;
- every oracle table entry and ordered query is recorded, with actor
  `adversary`, `simulator`, `verifier`, or `extractorReplay`;
- `Q1` is frozen from adversary calls when the first run halts; later verifier
  and extractor calls do not retroactively enter `Q1`;
- programming is a separate operation and aborts if the input was previously
  defined, even with the same output;
- fresh queries are controlled by a function of the complete ordered history
  and the current input;
- the original experiment shell can record an opaque restart checkpoint, but
  that field is only a game surface and is not used to justify K1.6;
- `V7FsStateRestorationCoupling` instead closes a start-only capability over
  one hidden random tape, computes the original execution from that closure,
  and derives each operational pause by replaying a concrete prefix; the
  extractor cannot read or replace the tape and is not handed an arbitrary
  adversary-memory checkpoint;
- oracle/program/query, fuel, runtime, restart, three grinding-query, q16
  branch, verifier, and simulated-proof budgets are explicit;
- a checked Markov inequality converts an expected natural-number cost into
  the explicit probability of exceeding a nonzero hard cutoff;
- acceptance, resource abort, programming abort, WUR failure, transcript
  coupling failure, and valid witness extraction are separate events; and
- the extractor structure contains only an algorithm. It has no
  `compiledBound`, `extract_sound`, or conclusion-shaped field.

Freshness has two levels. Ganesh et al.'s observed target requires pair
freshness `(instance, proof)`. A globally fresh attempt/proof-account ID is
strictly stronger, and Lean proves `AttemptFresh -> PairFresh`. Production
binds the proof account into the precommit and both root salts, but a finalized
account can be submitted again. Therefore deployed Tag 73 does **not** by
itself establish global attempt freshness relative to an arbitrary simulated
history. The observed-proof route needs FS-WUR unless the surrounding game
adds and proves that stronger freshness condition.

The older `ClassicalFSCompiler` is not an FS proof: its `compiledBound` member
is already the desired inequality. Likewise, the Pool
`KnowledgeExtractorInterface` only types an extractor boundary; a public proof
and one observed SHA query log do not construct the same-randomness black-box
replays required by BCS or Ganesh. Neither interface is used to discharge the
missing custom coupling.

### BCS 2016

[Ben-Sasson, Chiesa and Spooner, *Interactive Oracle Proofs*](https://eprint.iacr.org/2016/116.pdf):

- Section 2.3 defines the noninteractive proof-of-knowledge extractor as
  strict PPT, black-box, and same-randomness restarting.
- Definition 5.1 distinguishes state-restoration soundness from
  state-restoration **proof of knowledge**.
- Section 5.4 permits restricted restoration except returning to the empty
  verifier state after the first iteration.
- Lemma 7.3 supplies the ROM-to-state-restoration coupling loss.
- Theorem 7.1 gives, for a `Q`-query adversary,

  ```text
  e_FS-k(x,Q,lambda)
    = e_sr-k(x,Q) + 3(Q^2 + 1) / 2^lambda.
  ```

- Lemma 3.2 is only the ordinary full-output binary-Merkle extraction lemma;
  it does not cover two shared, typed, 208-bit trees.

This is the strongest relevant blueprint for a plain classical-ROM Tag-73
AoK, but the applicability gate proves that Tag-73 is not the literal output
of the Section-6 compiler. Ordinary soundness cannot replace `e_sr-k`, no
soundness probability is divided by adversary work, and the displayed BCS
constant cannot be copied until a Tag-specific analogue of Lemma 7.3 is
proved.

### Block et al. 2023/2024

[Block et al., *Fiat–Shamir Security of FRI and Related SNARKs*](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1071.pdf):

## Exact raw-error status

There is not yet a justified numerical Tag-73 compiler error. In particular,
the following must **not** be placed in the plain theorem merely because they
appeared in an earlier conditional ledger:

- the BCS `3(Q_RO^2+1)/2^256` term, because literal applicability fails;
- a `C2-after-lambda-chi` loss, because adaptive C2 is a legal later prover
  message in a multi-round IOP;
- three independent work-error terms, because the leading-zero predicates can
  be erased pointwise while retaining every nonce, query, and absorb;
- bounded-sampler exhaustion or all-q16-fail probabilities, because those are
  verifier rejection/honest-completeness events unless a coupling explicitly
  conditions them away; or
- simulator/WUR terms, because those belong only to the stronger observed-
  proof theorem.

For a concrete future Tag-specific coupling construction `C`, the exact plain
raw expression must instead be defined from its actual failure partition:

```text
εTag73,plain,raw(C) =
    εK1.2,two-tree-208
  + εK1.3,circle-list
  + εK1.4,coherent-chain
  + εK1.5,witness
  + Pr[C fails with an unqueried transcript-driving prediction]
  + Pr[C fails with a full-256 input/state/output collision]
  + Pr[C fails with a forward-reference or programming conflict]
  + Pr[C fails in the adaptive q16/query-DAG forest coupling]
  + Pr[C fails fixed-instance/attempt binding]
  + Pr[C exceeds its strict query/restart/runtime budget].
```

This is a raw union-bound expression and may exceed one. Some listed events
may later be merged or proved impossible, but that must follow from the
construction rather than bookkeeping. `Q_RO` must count every adversarial SHA
call in each run, including all three nonce-search families and discarded q16
branches. The K1.2 208-bit term stays separately typed and is never replaced
by a generic 104-bit birthday slogan.

The proved `FullOutput256` lemmas supply exact `1/2^256` and
`n/2^256` bounds after conditioning on a fixed finite target family. They do
not prove that the adaptive Tag-73 failure partition has three BCS-shaped
families. `three_quadratic_events_le_bcs_numerator` is only union arithmetic
from three separately supplied bounds.

Formally, `plainRomCompilerRawError` is

```text
Pr[unqueried transcript-driving prediction]
+ Pr[full-256 input/state/output collision]
+ Pr[forward reference or programming conflict]
+ Pr[adaptive q16/query-DAG forest failure]
+ Pr[fixed instance/attempt binding failure]
+ Pr[strict query/restart/runtime/timeout failure].
```

`plain_rom_compiler_failure_union_probability_le_raw_error` proves the union
bound without any disjointness or independence assumption. The four upstream
K1.2--K1.5 quantities are added separately when the interactive knowledge
theorem is instantiated.

If Block Theorem 3.15 is used instead, the upstream interactive error must
include its exact `Q_RO * epsilon_RBR-k`; it must not be silently added twice.

## Explicit K1.2–K1.5 premises

`UpstreamK12ToK15KnowledgeBound` may be instantiated only after supplying all
of the following for the legal interactive experiment:

1. K1.2: the two-tree, shared-topology, typed-leaf, 208-bit query-graph
   extractor and its exact failure event;
2. K1.3: the published circle-code/list-decoding theorem with every deployed
   rate, distance, multiplicity, and proximity side condition;
3. K1.4: selection of one coherent candidate across semantic, OOD, query,
   and four relation boundaries;
4. K1.5: deterministic recovery of a witness satisfying the deployed spend
   relation from that coherent chain; and
5. a bounded/expected-polynomial interactive state-restoration extractor with
   explicit oracle-query, restart, and runtime loss `L`.

None of these premises may be ordinary soundness. The K1.2–K1.5 theorem's
left side is `Pr[legal interactive execution]`, not `Pr[Fiat–Shamir accepts]`.

## Smallest remaining proof

For every bounded Tag-73 adversary, fix its auxiliary input and random tape.
In the joint first-run/replay world, and outside the explicitly named bad
events, prove all of the following in one trace reduction:

1. every deployed SHA call uses the same lazy programmable table;
2. `Q1` is exactly the first-run adversary history at halt;
3. replay starts from the same hidden tape, derives a nonempty adversary-
   program pause by replaying from start, and then maps that prefix to a
   complete previously seen verifier state; answers may depend on the ordered
   history and current query;
4. the program ID, release, statement digest, proof-account attempt, C1, then
   `lambda,chi`, then adaptive C2 are preserved byte for byte;
5. the proved bounded field/nonzero/circle/q16 decoders are coupled to the
   adaptive lazy-oracle raw blocks, including exact partial/exhaustion paths;
6. every 35/31/34-bit adversary probe, selected-nonce verifier check, and nonce
   absorb is preserved and charged at its actual saved state, while only the
   three leading-zero rejection predicates are erased pointwise;
7. all 64 q16 candidates clone the post-final-nonce state and the accepted
   branch is exactly the first cap-203 branch;
8. the two 208-bit typed authentication graphs and degree-15 `rho`-batched
   residual are the interactive verifier messages/checks expected by K1.2;
9. resource exhaustion maps to its exact raw event; and
10. outside those events, the query-DAG map proves—not merely labels—the
    resulting object a legal restricted state-restoration IOP execution.

No audited paper proves this statement for Tag 73. It is narrower than the
desired AoK inequality and mentions neither a witness nor extraction success,
so proving it does not assume the conclusion.

Canonical-simulator programming and WUR are intentionally absent from this
plain statement. They enter only a later observed-proof lifting.

## Minimal ways forward

The open proof-only route is to implement the trace adapter above over raw
256-bit random-oracle blocks and prove its coupling event by event. If that
custom adapter succeeds with the required legal-state and probability laws,
no deployed protocol change is needed for plain AoK; this work does not yet
prove that existence claim.

For observed proofs, one additional route is necessary:

- prove perfect-HVZK canonical simulation plus Tag-73 FS-WUR and programming
  freshness, then instantiate Ganesh Lemmas 3.1–3.2; or
- strengthen the application experiment/protocol so the target proof account
  is globally fresh relative to every simulated proof, and prove a new-domain
  separation lemma from the attempt absorption before C1. The deployed
  verifier alone does not enforce this history-relative property.

To invoke BCS literally without a custom adapter would require a material
protocol revision to the standard BCS full-output hash/Merkle chain and a
fixed unconditioned raw public-coin schedule. That is not claimed as necessary
for security; it is only the minimal route to verbatim theorem syntax.

## Trusted boundaries after this work

The intended final chain may keep only these cryptographic/system assumptions
external:

- SHA-256 as the required random oracle and collision/preimage primitive;
- Poseidon security in the upstream spend relation;
- the published circle-code/list-decoding theorem;
- Solana runtime/SBF correspondence; and
- pinned Charon/Aeneas translation correctness as a named trusted-tool
  boundary.

K1.2–K1.5 remain explicit modular premises while their dedicated work is in
progress. No `ClassicalFSCompiler.compiledBound`, public-proof-plus-one-query
decoder, conclusion-shaped axiom, `sorry`, or `admit` is introduced.
