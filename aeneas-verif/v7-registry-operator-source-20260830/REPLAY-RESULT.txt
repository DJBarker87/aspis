Hard mainnet boundary
---------------------

V1 receives no ProgramData account, hashes no executable payload and does not
authenticate an upgrade authority.  A frozen registry therefore does not make
an upgradeable selected verifier immutable.  The Lean result
current_v1_selection_is_independent_of_programdata_code_hash_and_authority
proves this omission is real rather than hiding it behind a theorem premise.

Mainnet activation remains blocked until V2, or an equivalently strong
immutable-deployment certificate, is implemented, source-bridged, measured
and exercised in the finalized devnet lifecycle.  The minimal V2 account,
state, compatibility, transaction-size and CU design is documented in
docs/research/v7-registry-operator-closure-20260830.md.
