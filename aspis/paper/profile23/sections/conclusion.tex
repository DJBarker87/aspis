\label{sec:conclusion}

Profile~23 combines an explicitly delimited private-state relation, a
circle-domain transparent argument, and a Solana account transition whose
verification and mutation occur in one transaction.  For the frozen
\(q=18,g=37\)
instance, the conservative classical-ROM analysis gives a 100.161-bit
work-normalized argument-soundness floor under the stated finite-parameter
and state-restoration premises.  Under the stated affine-image premise, the
declared programmable-ROM Proof-or-Abort view has a witness-free simulator with a 104.025-bit
real-versus-simulator floor and a 103.025-bit pairwise-witness floor.  The
released 66,367-byte proof verifies under a 915,656-byte SBF program, with the
worst measured local tag~60 path consuming 1,314,386 of 1,400,000 compute
units.

The devnet rehearsal executes the same released proof and program identity.
Its final transaction consumes \ProfileDevnetComputeUnits{} compute units,
creates the canonical
nullifier marker, and advances the pool sequence and anchor atomically.  The
finalized transaction and content-addressed evidence record make the implementation
claim independently inspectable while keeping it distinct from the theorem
scope.  Extending the construction beyond a same-path, one-input/one-output
transition, and establishing a separate mainnet deployment and authority
policy, remain future systems work.
