\section{Assumptions and claim boundary}
\label{sec:limitations}

The end-to-end theorem begins with a successful execution of the translated
deployed proof checker and ends with the complete evidence consumed by the
formal security classification.  The surrounding upload, account, cleanup,
compiler, and chain layers have their own specifications and artifact records.
Two open composition statements sit directly on the path to a deployed
probability theorem:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Statement identification.}  The translated entry path checks
        the live statement against its digest.  A field-by-field theorem must
        identify that object with the abstract statement used by the
        false-acceptance and theft experiments.
  \item \textbf{Experiment lift.}  The deterministic classification of one
        successful call must be embedded in the causal probability law used
        for work-normalized accounting, including oracle queries, rejection
        sampling, and repeated attempts.
\end{enumerate}

The accepted-execution theorem derives the mathematical consequences of
verifier success.  Separate completeness theorems cover the honest selector
and work schedule, while the archived proof supplies a complete successful
execution of the deployed program.

\subsection{Poseidon2 parameter status}

The construction uses Poseidon2 over \(\F\) with width sixteen, exponent five,
eight full rounds, and fourteen partial rounds
\cite{GrassiKhovratovichSchofnegger2023Poseidon2}.  Recent algebraic analysis
improves attacks on several Poseidon2 variants and recommends evaluating each
parameter set separately \cite{MerzRodriguezGarcia2026Poseidon2}.  That work
reports no full-round break for the recommended instances it studies and
places its ideal-degree estimates above \(2^{128}\) when the exponent is at
least three.  It does not publish a concrete advantage for the exact Aspis
tuple.  We therefore keep the Poseidon2 collision and preimage terms symbolic.

\subsection{Interpretation}

The formal result is end to end for the selected successful proof-checking
execution.  The main numerical statement is the conditional work-normalized
theorem of \cref{thm:conditional-soundness}; the raw statement is
\cref{eq:resource-bounded-advantage}.  The mainnet transaction in
\cref{sec:evaluation} identifies the binary, proof, cost, and state transition
used for evaluation.  These three statements have distinct scopes and share
the artifact links needed to audit their connections.
